Terms and Conditions

  1. The standard emergency loan principal is KSh 5,000.
  2. The interest on a KSh 5,000 emergency loan is KSh 1,000.
  3. The full amount payable at maturity is KSh 6,000.
  4. A first-loan processing fee of KSh 500 applies to the first loan.
  5. A renewal fee of KSh 500 applies when the client chooses to renew.
  6. In case of renewal, the client pays KSh 1,000 interest plus the KSh 500 renewal fee, making KSh 1,500.
  7. The scheduled repayment date is the same date of the following month as the borrowing date.
  8. Late repayment attracts an additional KSh 100 per day, added to the KSh 1,000 interest.
  9. The client must present the required signed agreement and identification documents to the selected agent.
  10. The original physical agreement and required identification documents are handled according to the Jitume Leo application and agent verification process.

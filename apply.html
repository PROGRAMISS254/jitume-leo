Terms & Conditions

Please read the proposed terms carefully.

By applying for a Jitume Leo emergency loan, the client acknowledges the following proposed terms for this prototype loan product:

  • Principal loan amount: KSh 5,000.
  • Loan interest: KSh 1,000.
  • The normal amount due at maturity is KSh 6,000.
  • The first loan has a KSh 500 processing fee.
  • A renewal carries a KSh 500 renewal fee and KSh 1,000 interest under the proposed prototype rules.
  • A late repayment charge of KSh 100 per late day is part of the proposed prototype rule.
  • The expected repayment date is the same calendar date in the following month, subject to the final agreement.
  • Agent verification and final admin approval are required before disbursement.

These terms are prototype business rules and must be reviewed and legally validated before real-world use.
